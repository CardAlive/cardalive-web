    Every pilot

Where we are

CardAlive is in development for Meta Quest 3. The prototypes show what the technology does today, with our own characters. It does not yet bring every existing game over in full. A pilot is how we find out what yours needs: we start with a small set of your cards, characters and mechanics, bring them into mixed reality, and decide together what comes next.
